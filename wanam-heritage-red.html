ABOUT THE FIRM

Wanam Sale Advocates is a Nairobi law firm advising individuals, families and businesses on civil, commercial, land, family and criminal matters.

Every matter is treated as its own file. Advice is built on verified statute, regulation and case law before it is given, and clients are kept informed at each stage.
