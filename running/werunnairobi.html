Your first run

Just show up. We'll handle the rest.

  • No sign up and no fee. Arrive before 7:30.
  • Pick 6, 9 or 15 km. Walk, jog or race, all paces run together.
  • Bring water and a phone. Leave the pressure at home.
  • Stay for coffee after. That's where the friendships start.

Our story

It started with four people and a coffee

Oct 2022

Emily posts on Instagram that she'll be waiting at 8 am for anyone who wants to run. Three women show up. A 6 km loop, then coffee.

2023 to 2024

Word spreads. The free Saturday run becomes the city's most visible running club.

First time?

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every Saturday run is free and there is no registration. Turn up, run, and stay for coffee if you like.

I'm not fast. Will I keep up?

All paces are welcome, and walkers too. Choose the 6 km route on your first Saturday and go at your own speed.

Where do we start?

The start point is shared each week. Follow us on Instagram or join the WhatsApp reminders so you never miss it.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, water and your phone. That is all.

Can my company host a run?

Yes. Use the partner form above and we'll get back to you.
